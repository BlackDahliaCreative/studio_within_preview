A Few Things to Know Before Your Visit

Booking and Cancellations

A valid credit or debit card is required on file to book any appointment or consultation. We kindly ask for at least 48 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Changes made within that window, including no shows, are subject to a charge of 100% of the reserved service total.

Children & Guests

To maintain a peaceful, relaxing environment, we kindly ask that guests attend their appointments alone. Children under 13 must be accompanied by an adult who is not receiving a service unless prior arrangements have been approved. Please note that curly cuts are priced by service, not age.

Your Experience

Your comfort and satisfaction are important to us! We offer complimentary beverages and light snacks during your visit. If you have any questions about our services or policies, we're always happy to help or answer any questions.

A Chicago Salon for Over 20 Years, Now Dedicated to Curls

Who we are

Studio Within is a curly hair salon in Chicago specializing in customized curl cuts, curly hair color, styling, and healthy curl care. For more than 20 years, Studio Within has continued to evolve, beginning as a full service salon and spa with private studio spaces and growing into the curl focused salon it is today. Located in Chicago’s Arcadia Terrace neighborhood, just north of Lincoln Square on Western Avenue near Bryn Mawr, our modern, open concept salon is designed around highly specialized care for curly and textured hair.

Owner Kristy has spent more than two decades in the salon industry as a stylist, salon owner, mentor, and business builder. As Studio Within became increasingly focused on curly hair, Kristy and the Studio Within team continued investing in advanced curl education, curl specific training, and certifications in curly cutting techniques.

Our curl specialists understand that no two heads of curls are the same. Every customized curly haircut is designed around the guest’s curl pattern, texture, density, shrinkage, lifestyle, styling routine, and hair goals. Our training goes beyond simply adapting traditional haircutting techniques for curly hair. We consider how curls naturally move, fall, respond to moisture, and behave between salon visits.

The Studio Within experience goes beyond the haircut, too. Guests can enjoy coffee or tea, steam shampoos, personalized curl services, thoughtful styling spaces that offer a little more privacy, and snacks while color processes. From consultation to finished style, every detail is designed to make our guests feel comfortable, understood, and cared for. And if you’re lucky, you may even get a visit from one of our two resident pugs.

Sustainability

Care that goes beyond the chair...

At Studio Within, caring for your hair also means caring about the world around us. Through our partnership with Green Circle Salons, we’re able to divert and repurpose up to 85–95% of salon waste, including foils, color tubes, hair, plastics, and excess color. It’s one small way we’re working to leave things a little better than we found them.
